# 15-minute workshop pre-flight

## 15 minutes before

- Open the exact ChatGPT account/workspace you will demonstrate.
- Verify Settings -> Data Controls and Settings -> Personalization -> Memory labels on this account. Do not rely on yesterday's screenshot.
- Open Projects, Plugins and Study Mode once so you know what this plan/workspace actually exposes.
- Put the browser at 100% zoom, close personal tabs/notifications and enable Do Not Disturb.
- Confirm projector resolution and that body text is readable from the back row.

## 10 minutes before

- Open a test room.
- Scan the QR with a phone on cellular data.
- Advance two slides.
- Send a test booklet.
- Confirm the presenter receives it.
- End and delete the test class.

## 5 minutes before

- Put a printed/onscreen safety line up before anyone opens ChatGPT: **No real student, parent, colleague, safeguarding, SEN, health, grade or unpublished assessment data in a personal/unapproved account.**
- Decide the local escalation person/route for privacy, safeguarding and assessment questions.
- Keep `OFFLINE-FALLBACK.md` open.

## Go/no-go

Do not run the phone-booklet collection if the QR is missing the secure room key, participant names appear in relay debugging as plaintext, or the school has not agreed that named participant reflections may be collected. Run the content in solo/projector mode instead.

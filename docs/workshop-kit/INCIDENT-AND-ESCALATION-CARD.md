# Incident & Escalation Card

## If someone has pasted sensitive school data into an unapproved AI tool

1. **Stop** - do not paste more information or “ask the AI to delete it”.
2. **Preserve what you need to report** - tool/account, time, type of data, approximate people affected, action taken. Do not spread the sensitive content in screenshots or group chats.
3. **Use the school's incident / data-breach route immediately.** Inform the IBP/privacy/DPO contact according to local policy.
4. **Follow provider/account deletion controls** only as instructed by the school; do not assume deletion eliminates every processing/retention obligation.
5. **Safeguarding overrides workshop convenience.** If the content concerns a safeguarding risk, use the safeguarding route immediately and do not continue analysing the case in a personal AI account.

## Escalate before use when

- real student identities or sensitive data are involved;
- a connected app could expose Drive/email/calendar/classroom data;
- AI contributes to grades, admissions, placement, discipline or other consequential decisions;
- the task involves emotion/behaviour inference;
- the legal/contractual basis is unclear;
- the vendor/tool is not on the approved list.

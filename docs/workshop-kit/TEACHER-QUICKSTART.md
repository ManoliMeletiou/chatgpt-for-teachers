# Teacher Quickstart - ChatGPT at school

## Before the prompt

Ask five questions:

1. **Approval:** Is this account/tool approved for this school purpose?
2. **Data:** Am I including a real person or anything sensitive/confidential?
3. **Need:** Does the tool actually need this information?
4. **Consequence:** Could the output materially affect a student's opportunity, assessment, wellbeing or safeguarding?
5. **Verification:** How will I check the output independently?

If you cannot answer those confidently, stop and use your school route.

## Green / Amber / Red

**Green** - synthetic/public/non-personal inputs; low-stakes drafting; teacher checks.  
**Amber** - internal materials, parent/staff communication, assessment support, connected apps, uncertain approval. Check policy/data scope first.  
**Red** - named/sensitive student information, safeguarding, health/SEN, passwords, unpublished high-stakes tests, emotional inference, or AI making consequential student decisions. Do not put this in a personal/unapproved account.

## SCOPE-V prompt

- **Situation** - class/goal/context, without personal data.
- **Constraints** - age/level/time/curriculum/format/boundaries.
- **Output** - exactly what you want produced.
- **Persona** - useful role, not fake authority.
- **Examples** - show the standard/style where helpful.
- **Verification** - require checks, uncertainties and sources where appropriate.

## Teacher QA before use

Check: factual accuracy · mathematical/scientific correctness · curriculum fit · reading load · bias/stereotypes · accessibility · answer key · source/copyright needs · whether the task still measures the intended learning.

## Two account habits

On a personal account, review **Settings -> Data Controls -> Improve the model for everyone** and your **Memory** settings. These are data/personalisation controls; they do not make an otherwise unapproved school use approved. Temporary Chat can reduce persistence/personalisation for suitable low-risk work, but it is not a lawful-basis or school-policy switch.

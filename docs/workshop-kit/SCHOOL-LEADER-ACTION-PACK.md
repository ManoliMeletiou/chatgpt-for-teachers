# School Leader Action Pack - after the workshop

A good workshop should change school practice, not only individual prompting.

## Within 7 days

- Name an owner for AI governance and an operational contact for teachers.
- Publish one page: approved tools/accounts, prohibited data, who to ask, how to report an incident.
- Identify whether the school is using personal, managed, Edu/Business/Enterprise or other accounts and what that changes.
- Stop staff from assuming “free + training off” equals procurement/privacy approval.

## Within 30 days

- Map AI use cases by risk and data type.
- Decide which use cases are Green / Amber / Red locally.
- Review processor/vendor agreements, data flows, retention, sub-processors, connected apps and any required DPIA/FRIA/legal assessment.
- Align assessment/integrity guidance with actual subject practice.
- Define a safeguarding rule that keeps disclosures and case details out of unapproved generative AI.
- Run the school's IBP self-evaluation/roadmap work; do not build a separate “AI policy island”.

## Evidence to keep

Keep governance evidence, not unnecessary personal workshop data:

- approved tool register and decision owner;
- risk/use-case register;
- staff training date/materials;
- privacy/security review;
- incident route;
- assessment guidance;
- review date;
- action owner and status.

## Review triggers

Review policy when the provider changes data controls, adds a connected app, changes retention/training terms, retires/replaces a feature, or when the school changes from personal to managed workspaces.

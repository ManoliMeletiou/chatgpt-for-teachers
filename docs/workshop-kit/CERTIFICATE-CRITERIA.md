# Certificate Criteria

The certificate is a **certificate of completion/participation**, not OpenAI certification, legal accreditation, school approval or proof of independent competence.

Recommended minimum evidence before issuing:

- participant joined/completed the relevant live route or solo route;
- completed the core safety/data classification activity;
- produced or revised one SCOPE-V prompt;
- completed one verification/error-hunt activity;
- submitted the closing booklet/takeaway and 30-day action.

The current app makes the certificate available after a booklet is sent. Facilitators should not describe that automation as an exam result.

# Accessibility & Inclusion Checklist

## Slides / projector

- Prefer one idea per view and readable body text over dense “complete” slides.
- Read essential visual information aloud.
- Do not encode Green/Amber/Red meaning by colour alone; use the label and action.
- Avoid rapid auto-advance, flashing media and unnecessary animation.
- Allow participants to use the full booklet rather than forcing tiny inline fields.

## Activities

- Give instructions both orally and on screen.
- Offer individual thinking time before pair discussion.
- Use synthetic scenarios that do not ask participants to disclose personal experience.
- Allow keyboard-only interaction and visible focus.
- Do not treat fast prompting as AI competence; verification and judgement matter more.

## AI outputs

Ask ChatGPT to support accessibility only as a **drafting aid**. A shorter text is not automatically accessible. Check language, structure, cognitive load, alt text, reading order and the learner's actual needs; do not put a learner's diagnosis or support plan into an unapproved account.
